# G-Fone F1 — Beta 6.3.6 (Engineering Edition)

> Concept. Not a product. Licenses: CERN-OHL-P v2 / kernel GPLv2-only / userspace GPLv3 / Lineage Apache 2.0 / CC BY 4.0.

## Main Specifications

- **SoC:** Dimensity 9600 Pro, 2 nm. LPDDR6 + UFS 5.0.
- **Display:** 6.77" LTPS IPS QHD.
- **Battery:** 5S1P, 46.25 Wh. **X-Cross**; active BMS; shunt + per-cell NTC.
- **Charging:** 80 W **wired**. **No wireless charging** — efficiency many times worse; coil EMF **ruins the compass**; strong local heat is **unsafe for the 5S1P pack**. Half-joke: *under a burner, nothing living remains* — wireless is a slower, marketed version of the same physics.
- **Connectivity:** **no mechanical SIM/microSD**. **eSIM only**. **eSIM fully independent of ecosystems** (Apple/Google/OEM): carrier profiles, no store/cloud lock-in.
- **External storage:** USB host, any size. **USB → SATA with power** in the box.
- **Chassis:** 7075-T6; steel ring magnetic cooler; **ventilation texture** (directional micro-channels, not through-holes).

## Beta 6.3.6 Status

No wireless charging (efficiency, compass, pack heat); eSIM ecosystem-independent. (from 6.3.5: no SIM/SD slots, USB→SATA).
