# CHANGELOG

History of changes to the G-Fone F1 concept. Early versions (before Beta 3) are not published.

## Beta 6.3.7

- **USB Host Storage: User-Chosen Flexibility** — no microSD; any USB SSD; USB→SATA+power in box; tool, not appliance.
- **eSIM: Privacy-First Control** — activate/deactivate only; delete/modify/telemetry blocked without explicit consent; local immutable log; no hidden operator actions.
- **Why No microSD?** — standards (USB), repairability, 256 GB UFS 4.0 internal only.

## Beta 6.3.6

- **No wireless charging** — efficiency; compass EMF; pack heat (*under a burner, nothing living remains*).
- **eSIM fully independent of ecosystems** (Apple/Google/OEM).

## Beta 6.3.5

- **No mechanical SIM or microSD slots** — **eSIM only**.
- External drives any size via USB host; **USB → SATA with power** in the box.

## Earlier

See full history in the repository. Beta 1–2 not published.
