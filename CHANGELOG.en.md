# CHANGELOG

History of changes to the G-Fone F1 concept. Early versions (before Beta 3) are not published.

## Beta 6.3.6

- **No wireless charging** — rationale: far worse efficiency; EMF ruins the compass; strong heat is unsafe for the 5S1P pack (half-joke: *under a burner, nothing living remains*).
- **eSIM fully independent of ecosystems** (Apple/Google/OEM).

## Beta 6.3.5

- **No mechanical SIM or microSD slots** — **eSIM only**.
- **External drives of any size** via USB host (USB-C).
- **In the box:** **USB → SATA adapter with power** (2.5″/3.5″ SATA).

## Beta 6.3.4

- Pack-current **shunt**; **battery temperature sensors** (NTC on each of 5 cells).

## Beta 6.3.3

- **PTC film removed**; active BMS rationale (local heat / efficiency).

## Beta 6.3.2

- **Active balancing** as required baseline.

## Earlier

See full history in repository. Beta 1–2 not published.
