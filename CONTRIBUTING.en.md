# CONTRIBUTING

Thank you for your interest in G-Fone F1. This is a concept, not a product. Contributions are welcome, but there are rules.

## What is allowed

- **CAD and mechanics:** chassis, fins, X-Cross mounting, case, tripod.
- **Thermal simulations:** VC, gradients, fin efficiency, MEMS micropumps.
- **Software:** LineageOS-based, drivers, eBPF filters, UnifiedPush, BMS firmware.
- **BMS:** 5S1P balancing scheme, protection, coulomb counter.
- **Documentation:** translations, corrections, repair guides.
- **Tests:** prototypes, measurements, comparisons.

## What is not allowed

- Suggestions for circumventing patents.
- Calls to violate laws of any jurisdiction.
- Specific blacklists of apps or companies.
- Political statements and discussions of a political nature.
- Publication of third-party patents, NDA documentation, or closed datasheets.

## Contribution license

Depending on the type of contribution:

- **Hardware** (CAD, schematics, gerbers): CERN-OHL-P v2. Or shorter: attribute the author.
- **Software** (code, firmware, scripts): GPLv3.
- **Documentation** (texts, translations, guides): CC BY 4.0. Or shorter: attribute the author. Attribution template is in the CC BY 4.0 document.

By submitting a contribution, you agree to these licenses and confirm that you have the right to publish it.

## Code of Conduct

- Respect for other contributors.
- No politics, no toxicity, no aggression.
- Criticism must be constructive and argument-based. Always verify AI-generated arguments before submitting.
- Violation of the code of conduct results in a block without warning.

## Process

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a branch for the task (`feat/thermal-sim`, `fix/bms-balance`).
3. Pull Request with description: what, why, what risks.
4. Review by the maintainer.
5. Merge.

## Disclaimer

All contributions are published as part of the concept. The author is not responsible for their use, patent consequences, or regulatory violations. No FTO audit has been performed. Users are responsible for compliance with the laws of their jurisdiction.
